Ogden, Kansas

Lawn care in Ogden, Kansas.

Ogden is the small city immediately west of Manhattan, in Riley County. It is part of the Manhattan side of the route, with the full service list.

Close to Manhattan, listed on its own

Ogden is easy to treat as a neighborhood of Manhattan, and that would be sloppy. It is its own city, on the Kansas River just west of town, and it is one of the six places KS Lawn Ranger names as a core service area. Because it sits on the Manhattan side of the map, a mowing route that is already in town can include Ogden without a special trip from Alma. You do not need a different company for a smaller city next door.

Lots are mostly residential. Some are older and shaded, and the ground near the river can hold moisture the way other valley lawns do. If the yard stays soft after rain, say so. We would rather schedule the cut for a drier day than leave ruts. Weekly and bi-weekly mowing, edging, and blow-off are the standing services. Mulching is standard. Bagging is available if you ask for it in the quote.

Beds, leaves, and new turf

Shaded Ogden yards collect leaves and grow thin turf under trees. Fall leaf removal with haul-away keeps that off the grass. Aeration and overseeding in September and October are how thin areas get thicker. Bed weeding and a mulch or rock install are available when the foundation plantings need more than a mow. Sod is available when a section is bare rather than thin.

Snow on a short drive

Residential snow removal in Ogden is usually a driveway, a walk, and steps — the same scope as a Manhattan house. A seasonal plan is worthwhile if you want the address on the route before a storm. There is no bare-pavement guarantee. Ice melt can be part of the visit. If you run a business in Ogden, mark the form Commercial and look at commercial snow.

Ogden does not require the “send your address so we can confirm” step that Westmoreland and Paxico do. It is a core city. Call or text (913) 221-7981, Monday through Saturday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. The company is fully insured, proof on request, and based in Alma.
