A different town than Manhattan, on purpose

Junction City sits where the Smoky Hill and Republican rivers meet, next to Fort Riley. It is larger and more spread out than Wamego or St. George, and it has a real mix of houses and commercial property. KS Lawn Ranger serves it as one of six core cities. We do not claim a contract on Fort Riley, and we do not imply one. If your property is in town — a house, a rental, a storefront, an office — it can be quoted like any other stop.

The drive from Alma is longer than the drive to Wamego, which is why Junction City is named explicitly instead of being folded into “and surrounding areas.” It is on the route. Send the address and the service, and the quote accounts for the property itself.

Homes, rentals, and businesses

Residential work is mowing and edging, bed maintenance, shrub trimming, seasonal cleanup with haul-away, and the install work — mulch, rock, sod, landscaping, patios, and walls — when a property needs more than a cut. Rentals are still residential. If you need a yard brought back between tenants, describe that in the message. It is a cleanup and a mowing quote, not a special package with a made-up name.

Businesses should mark the form Commercial. A weekly mowing route keeps a property from being called only when the grass is embarrassing. Bed maintenance keeps the front planting from going wild between those cuts. Invoices are available and billing can run through QuickBooks. Proof of insurance is available on request, which is the normal ask from a commercial client.

Snow contracts

Junction City businesses that need walks and entrances open all winter should ask for a seasonal snow contract before the season, not during the first storm. Homes use residential snow removal. Ice melt can be included. Bare pavement is not guaranteed, for the same weather reasons as everywhere else we work.
