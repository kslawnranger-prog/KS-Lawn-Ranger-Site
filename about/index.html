What the company actually does

The work covers the year. In the growing season that is weekly or bi-weekly mowing and edging, bed weeding, and shrub trimming. Spring and fall add leaf removal and haul-away. Install work includes mulch, decorative rock, sod, landscaping, and hardscaping such as patios and retaining walls. Fall is when aeration and overseeding are scheduled. Winter is snow removal and ice management for homes and for commercial properties, including a seasonal snow contract if you want to be on the route before the first storm.

Mulching mowers are the standard finish. Bagging can be added to a quote. Core-aeration plugs are left on the lawn to break down. Snow service does not promise bare pavement, because refreeze, drifting, and traffic can put ice back on a surface after a visit.

How to hire the company

Estimates are free. Most of them start with the address and a look at the property, because lot size, obstacles, and haul-away change the job. You can call or text (913) 221-7981, email kslawnranger@gmail.com, or use the form. Say whether the property is a home or a business, and pick phone, text, or email as the way you want the reply.

Hours are Monday through Saturday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Sunday is closed. Payment is cash, check, Venmo, or a QuickBooks payment link. Invoices are available on request, and estimates and billing are handled in QuickBooks. The company is fully insured, and proof is available on request.
