Hardscaping

Patios and retaining walls.

Hardscaping here means patios and retaining walls, built to the scope in the quote. It is project work, looked at on site before a price is given.

Get a Free Quote Call (913) 221-7981 Text

The two jobs this page covers

Patios and retaining walls are the hardscaping KS Lawn Ranger installs. A patio is the flat work: pavers or a similar surface where you actually sit or walk. A retaining wall holds a change in grade so a slope becomes a usable edge. Project photos on this site show a paver patio beside river rock, and a stone retaining wall with a rock bed finished in front of it. Those are the kinds of jobs this service refers to.

Every site is different. Slope, access for materials, what is already in the ground, and how the patio has to meet a walk or a door all change the job. That is why there is no price on this page and no “starting at” number. The quote comes after an on-site look.

What to have ready

Send the address and say whether you are thinking about a patio, a wall, or both. Photos of the slope or the area you want paved help the first conversation. If other work belongs in the same project — a rock bed, mulch, or plants where the wall meets the yard — check landscaping on the form so it is one quote instead of two trips to talk about it.

Old concrete, failed edging, and excavated soil are hauled away when haul-away is written into the quote. Do not assume a dumpster or disposal is included until you see it listed.

What this service is not

This is not an engineering stamp, a permit service, or a promise about drainage laws. If your city requires a permit for a wall or a patio, that is the property owner’s item to confirm. We build the hardscape described in the quote. Drainage is discussed on site when a slope is part of the reason for the wall, and the scope you approve is the scope that gets built.
