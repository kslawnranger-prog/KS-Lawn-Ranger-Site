Two kinds of customers, two pages

Snow work splits for a reason. A house needs the driveway, the front walk, the steps, and a path to the mailbox. A business needs the entrance customers and employees actually use, often on a contract that was signed before the season so the property is not scrambling after the first inch. Both are available. What ice management means here

Clearing snow is half of it. Ice melt or salt can be added per visit or written into the plan. Pre-treatment — putting material down before a storm when the forecast supports it — is available by request. It is not automatic. Ask for it when you want it, and it goes in the quote.

We do not guarantee bare pavement. Weather changes after we leave. Surfaces refreeze. Snow drifts back. Cars pack what is left into ice. The work is to clear what is present at the time of service and open safe access. Anyone who promises a bare, dry lot for the entire day after a Kansas storm is selling something this company does not sell.

Get on the list before the storm

The useful time to call is before you need the service. A seasonal plan lets us know the address, the surfaces, and whether you want ice melt, so the route is built with you on it. A per-visit request still works, and it is how some homes prefer to handle a light year. Either way, send the address now. Hours in season are still Monday through Saturday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
