Install work, not a maintenance visit

Weekly mowing keeps a lawn where it is. A landscaping install changes the yard: new or rebuilt beds, mulch or decorative rock, sod where the turf is gone, and plantings when you want something in the bed besides mulch. KS Lawn Ranger quotes that work on site. Tell us what you like — mulch or rock, a low-maintenance bed, a place where the lawn should stop — and the quote follows the property, not a package from a menu.

Plant installs can be part of the same quote as prep and mulch. The existing service already includes that. We are not a landscape architecture office and we do not publish design fees or drawings. You get an on-site estimate, a clear scope, and the installation. Waste and old material are hauled when the quote includes haul-away.

How an install is usually sequenced

  • You send the address and a short description, or photos of the area.
  • We look at access, the shape of the beds, and what has to come out.
  • The quote names the work: edging, mulch or rock, sod, plants, and haul-away if you want it.
  • The job is scheduled Monday through Saturday between 7:00 AM and 6:00 PM.

Hardscaping — patios and retaining walls — is related and has its own page, because the labor and the materials are different from a bed of mulch. If the project needs both, one form submission is enough. Check landscaping and hardscaping.

After the install

New beds still need weeding. New sod needs water from you while it knits. If you want the lawn on a weekly route after the project, check that box too. Bed maintenance can follow an install so the edge we cut does not disappear by July. None of those follow-ups are assumed. They are on the quote if you ask for them.

The company is fully insured, proof on request. Payment is cash, check, Venmo, or a QuickBooks payment link.
