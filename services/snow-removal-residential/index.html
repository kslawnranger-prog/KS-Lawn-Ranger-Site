Residential snow

Snow removal for your home.

Driveways, front walks, steps, and the short path that makes a house usable after a storm. One visit at a time, or a seasonal plan.

The surfaces that matter at a house

Residential snow removal is about getting a household in and out. That is the driveway, the sidewalk in front, the walk to the door, the steps, and often a path to a mailbox or a side door you actually use. It is not a blank check to clear every inch of a rural acreage. The quote names the surfaces. If a drive is steep, narrow, or shared, say so. Those details change how the visit goes and they are easier to hear before a storm than during one.

The photo on this page is a house driveway opened up with snow piled to the side. That is the result people are asking for: a place to park and a way to the door. Ice can still be present. See the note on bare pavement below.

Per visit or a seasonal plan

Some homes only call after a storm that is bigger than they want to shovel. That is a per-visit clear, and it depends on the route having room that day. A seasonal plan means the address is already known, the surfaces are agreed, and you are not introducing the property in the middle of a snowfall. Check “Seasonal snow contract” if that is what you want. The form defaults this page toward a home. Switch it if we have the wrong property type.

Ice melt or salt can be added to any visit or included every time. Pre-treatment is by request when the weather allows, not an automatic extra. Tell us if the household includes someone who needs the walk open early, and we will talk about where that property sits on the route. We will not invent a clock-time guarantee on the website.

Limits, in plain language

We do not guarantee bare pavement. Refreeze overnight, wind, and tires will put slick spots back. Service clears what is on the ground at the time we are there and opens access. If you need a different standard, it has to be a conversation, not an assumption.

This service covers the six core towns: Manhattan, Alma, Wamego, St. George, Junction City, and Ogden. If you are in Westmoreland or Paxico, send the address and we will confirm.
